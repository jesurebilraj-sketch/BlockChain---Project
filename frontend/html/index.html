The Challenge

Traditional PDS Needs a New Layer of Trust.

Manual Records

Fragmented records make verification difficult.

Limited Visibility

Stakeholders cannot easily trace the journey of supplies.

Data Manipulation

Centralized records create opportunities for unauthorized changes.

Delayed Auditing

Manual verification makes auditing slow and expensive.

The Solution

From Distribution to Verification.

Immutable Records

Every approved transaction is cryptographically linked.

End-to-End Traceability

Track movement from warehouse to Fair Price Shop to beneficiary.

Federated Verification

Authorized validator nodes independently verify transactions.

Transparent Auditing

Transactions can be verified without modifying historical records.

Process

One Supply. Five Verified Stages.

Warehouse

Stock is received and recorded at source.

Fair Price Shop

Supplies are transferred to local distribution points.

Beneficiary

Entitlements are issued directly to citizens.

Blockchain

The transaction is validated and permanently recorded.

Audit

Auditors verify the trail without altering history.

The Ledger

Every Transaction Becomes Part of the Record.

Each block links to the one before it, so history can be read but never rewritten.
