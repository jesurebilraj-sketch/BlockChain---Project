Why Blockchain for PDS?

Overcoming Challenges in Traditional Distribution.

Paper & Siloed Logs

Paper registers and fragmented central databases cause reconciliation bottlenecks and human errors.

Grain Diversion & Leakage

Stock passing through multiple logistics points is vulnerable to unrecorded diversion and adulteration.

Data Tampering

Centralized servers can be vulnerable to unauthorized record alterations and ghost beneficiary inflation.

Delayed Audit Trail

Reconciling national grain distributions takes months rather than real-time automated verification.

The PDSChain Architecture

How PDSChain Solves It.

Cryptographic Ledger

Every bag of grain moved is timestamped, hashed, and chained permanently into an immutable distributed ledger.

End-to-End Tracking

Track grains seamlessly: Central Depot → Regional Warehouse → Fair Price Shop → Citizen Beneficiary.

Federated Consensus (FBA)

Decentralized quorum slices across Civil Supplies, State Commissions, and oversight bodies guarantee consensus.

Citizen Instant Verification

Citizens and inspectors can independently verify rations using cryptographic transaction hashes.

End-to-End Workflow

Citizen → FPS → Txn → Validators → Consensus → Blockchain → Verification

1. Citizen

Authenticates entitlement via Beneficiary ID at local shop.

2. Fair Price Shop

Weighs grains and generates cryptographically signed distribution.

3. Transaction

Broadcasts payload with stock updates to the validator pool.

4. Validators (FBA)

12 authorized nodes vote across overlapping quorum slices.

5. Verified Block

Transaction anchored into block and verifiable by anyone.
